# Map: every Item in the field

01. Energy Search & Energy Retrieval — a Basic Energy from Library or Discard
02. Energy Recycler — a `Destination` that shuffles into the Library
03. Team Rocket's Transceiver — a Supporter filtered by a name substring
04. Enhanced Hammer — discard a Special Energy from an opponent's Pokémon
05. Tool Scrapper — discard Tools from either side of the board
06. Hand Trimmer — both players discard down to 5, opponent first
07. Secret Box — one of each Trainer kind, paid for in hand
08. Glass Trumpet & Tera Orb — refused: no Tera concept in the artifact
09. Dusk Ball — read the bottom of the Library
10. Prime Catcher — a two-sided switch
11. Strange Timepiece — devolve a Pokémon, and block its evolving this turn
12. Transformation Tome — a cost paid in a second copy of itself
