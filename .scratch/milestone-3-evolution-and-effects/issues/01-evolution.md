# Evolution

Type: task
Status: ready-for-human

Add evolution. Rules 19 to 22 are written and unimplemented, and both
committed decks are built on Stage 1 and Stage 2 lines.

This one is Nick's. It needs no effect system, the rules are already stated,
and the engine has every part it depends on.

- [ ] A Pokémon evolves from the card it names, and only from that card
- [ ] It must have been in play since the beginning of the turn, and cannot
      evolve the turn it was played
- [ ] It cannot evolve twice in one turn, and neither player evolves on the
      first turn of the game
- [ ] It keeps its damage and its attachments, and loses every Special
      Condition
- [ ] Evolution works on the Active and on a Benched Pokémon
