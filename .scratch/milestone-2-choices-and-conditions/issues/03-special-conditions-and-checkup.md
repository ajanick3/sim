# Special conditions and checkup

Type: task
Status: claimed

Add the five Special Conditions and the Pokémon Checkup between turns.

- [ ] Only the Active can carry a condition
- [ ] Asleep, Paralyzed, and Confused replace each other; Burned and Poisoned do not
- [ ] The checkup runs conditions first, then knocks out anything at 0 HP
- [ ] A player chooses the order of their own effects in the checkup
