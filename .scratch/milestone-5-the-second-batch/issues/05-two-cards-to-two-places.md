# Two cards to two places

Type: task
Status: ready-for-agent

`Crispin` searches for up to 2 Basic Energy of **different types**, puts one
in hand, and attaches the other. 67 slots.

Three things `Phase::Deciding` has never had to do: send two searched cards to
two different places, hold a constraint between two choices, and attach
directly from a search.

- [ ] A search can send its cards to more than one destination
- [ ] A constraint between two choices is expressible, and is a value
